# Name Options

## Recommended: WellMind Health
**Tagline:** Understand. Prevent. Heal. Thrive.

Why it works:
- Broad enough for mental and general health.
- “WellMind” connects mental wellbeing with whole-person health.
- Easy to extend into training, campaigns and resources.
- Calm and trustworthy brand character.

## Alternatives
1. **Mind & Well Health** — broad and friendly.
2. **BlueWell Health** — strong visual brand potential.
3. **Wellness & Mind** — approachable but more wellness-oriented.
4. **Health Harmony** — emphasizes whole-person wellbeing.
5. **MindBody Wellbeing** — very descriptive.
6. **Wellbeing Compass** — strong educational/navigation metaphor.
7. **HealthWise Living** — practical and educational.
8. **ThriveWell Health** — optimistic and memorable.
9. **WholeMind Health** — holistic, mental-health-forward.
10. **Healthy Balance Hub** — descriptive and community-friendly.

Before final selection, verify trademark, domain, social-handle and regulatory considerations in the intended market.
