### 6. Training
Create structured learning content with learning objectives, lessons, practical exercises, quizzes and knowledge checks.

## Article formula
1. Clear headline
2. Short answer/summary
3. Why the topic matters
4. Evidence-informed explanation
5. Practical actions
6. What not to do
7. When to seek help
8. Expert/medical review
9. References
10. Related resources

## Editorial standards
- Prefer plain language.
- Explain technical terms.
- Separate evidence from opinion.
- State uncertainty.
- Date time-sensitive claims.
- Avoid sensational headlines.
- Correct errors transparently.
- Cite authoritative sources.
