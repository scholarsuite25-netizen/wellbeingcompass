# Wellbeing Compass — Understand. Prevent. Heal. Thrive.

## Official Product Name
**Wellbeing Compass**

**Tagline:** *Understand. Prevent. Heal. Thrive.*  
**Secondary Tagline:** *Navigate Life. Protect Health. Find Balance.*

Wellbeing Compass is a trusted digital health navigation, education, and public awareness platform focused on mental health, general vitality, preventive living, healthy relationships, social wellbeing, and environmental factors that shape human sanity and flourishing.

> **Clinical Governance Notice:** Wellbeing Compass is an evidence-informed educational and navigation resource. It does not provide medical diagnosis, emergency care, or individualized prescription therapy.

---

## Core Mission
To guide, educate, counsel, train, and empower people with credible, compassionate, and actionable health knowledge while helping them navigate the relationship, workplace, family, and environmental determinants of lifelong wellbeing.

---

## Brand Philosophy & Visual Identity
- **Name:** Wellbeing Compass — guiding individuals and families through complex health decisions with clarity and direction.
- **Primary Color:** Deep Navy & Medical Blue (`#0D2A4A`, `#1565C0`) — trust, clinical authority, calm, and security.
- **Accent Color:** Warm Gold & Amber (`#F59E0B`, `#FBBF24`) — hope, optimism, energy, and warmth.
- **Iconography:** The Compass Rose intertwined with heart and health indicators.
- **Tone:** Compassionate, evidence-informed, plain-language, culturally authentic, and non-judgmental.

---

## Primary Audiences
- General public & health-conscious readers
- Young adults and students seeking emotional coping skills
- Parents and caregivers nurturing child and adolescent wellbeing
- Couples and families building communication and trust
- Workplace leaders and employees preventing burnout
- Communities seeking public health awareness campaigns

---

## Primary Content Directions
1. **Mental Health & Emotional Sanity** (Anxiety, depression, coping toolkits)
2. **General Health & Vitality** (Sleep, nutrition, body wellness)
3. **Prevention & Healthy Habits** (Cardiovascular screening, risk reduction)
4. **Relationships & Boundaries** (Communication, conflict repair, empathy)
5. **Family & Parenting** (Adolescent resilience, mindful caregiving)
6. **Workplace Wellbeing** (Burnout prevention, psychological safety)
7. **Environment & Health** (Clean air, water, environmental wellness)
8. **Social Wellbeing & Belonging** (Loneliness, community support)
9. **Compass Academy & Training** (Guided wellbeing courses with progress tracking)
10. **Public Health Campaigns** (Mindful May, Healthy Hearts community initiatives)

---

## Technology Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router, Server & Client Components)
- **Language & Styling:** TypeScript, Vanilla Tailwind CSS (no component frameworks)
- **Database & ORM:** Prisma ORM with SQLite (local) / PostgreSQL (production on Vercel)
- **Authentication:** NextAuth.js (Auth.js v4) with bcrypt password hashing & JWT
- **Role-Based Access Control:** 14 distinct roles with clinical review gates
- **Deployment Ready:** GitHub repository & Vercel deployment with automatic Prisma client generation
