# WellMind Health — Medical & Mental Health Blog

## Proposed product name
**WellMind Health**

**Tagline:** *Understand. Prevent. Heal. Thrive.*

WellMind Health is a trusted digital health education and awareness platform focused on mental health, general health, healthy living, relationships, social wellbeing, and environmental factors that influence health and sanity.

> **Important:** WellMind Health is an educational and awareness platform, not a replacement for diagnosis, emergency care, or individualized treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.

## Core mission
To educate, counsel, train, and empower people with credible, compassionate, practical health information while addressing the environmental, relationship, workplace, family, and social factors that affect wellbeing.

## Brand philosophy
- Primary colour: Blue — trust, calm, competence, safety.
- Accent: Yellow — optimism, hope, energy, attention.
- Visual language: clean, reassuring, modern, human, medically credible, non-alarming.
- Tone: compassionate, evidence-informed, plain-language, non-judgmental.

## Primary audiences
- General public
- Young adults and students
- Parents and caregivers
- Couples and families
- Teachers and educators
- Employers and workplace leaders
- Community organisations
- Health-conscious readers
- People seeking mental-health awareness and practical coping education

## Primary content pillars
1. Mental Health
2. General Health
3. Prevention & Healthy Living
4. Relationships & Family
5. Social Wellbeing
6. Environment & Health
7. Workplace Wellbeing
8. Parenting & Child Wellbeing
9. Health Education & Training
10. Public Health & Awareness

## Safety principle
Every health article must distinguish education from diagnosis or treatment, use qualified review where appropriate, identify urgent warning signs, and direct readers to professional or emergency help when indicated.
