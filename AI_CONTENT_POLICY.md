# AI-Assisted Content Policy

AI may assist with:
- Outlines
- Drafting
- Plain-language editing
- SEO suggestions
- Metadata
- Content classification
- Internal research organization

AI must not independently determine:
- A user's diagnosis
- A user's treatment
- A medication dose
- Whether an emergency is occurring
- Whether a medical claim is safe to publish

Human editorial review is required for publishable medical content.

AI-generated citations, statistics, quotations and expert credentials must be independently verified before publication.

Never fabricate:
- Studies
- Doctors
- Hospitals
- Testimonials
- Patient stories
- Clinical results
- Statistics
