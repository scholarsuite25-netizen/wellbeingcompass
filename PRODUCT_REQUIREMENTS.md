# Product Requirements Document

## Product
WellMind Health

## Vision
A trustworthy digital publication and learning platform that helps people understand health, protect their wellbeing, make informed decisions, improve relationships and recognize when professional support is needed.

## Objectives
- Increase health literacy.
- Normalize constructive mental-health conversations.
- Promote prevention.
- Explain complex health information simply.
- Address social and environmental determinants of health.
- Provide practical education and training.
- Build a trusted health-information brand.

## Success metrics
- Returning readers
- Article completion/read depth
- Search success rate
- Newsletter subscriptions
- Course completion
- Content review compliance
- Accessibility compliance
- Core Web Vitals
- Organic search growth
- Reader feedback quality
- Correction rate
- Medical-review coverage

## Non-goals
- Diagnosing users
- Prescribing medication
- Replacing clinicians
- Operating as an emergency service
- Making unsupported therapeutic claims
