# Editorial Workflow

## Standard workflow
1. Pitch
2. Assignment
3. Research
4. Draft
5. Editorial review
6. Medical review when required
7. Fact checking
8. Copy editing
9. SEO review
10. Final approval
11. Publish
12. Post-publication monitoring
13. Scheduled re-review

## Risk levels
### Low
General wellness and lifestyle education.

### Moderate
Condition explainers, mental-health topics, medication-adjacent information, vulnerable audiences.

### High
Emergency symptoms, suicide/self-harm, abuse, severe disease, pregnancy complications, medication instructions, child safety.

High-risk content requires enhanced review and stronger help-seeking guidance.

## Re-review
Time-sensitive medical content should have an explicit review date. The CMS should alert editors when content becomes due for review.
