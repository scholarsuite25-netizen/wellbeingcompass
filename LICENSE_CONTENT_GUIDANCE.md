# Content, Image & Licensing Guidance

Every image must have a known usage basis:
- Owned/commissioned
- Properly licensed
- Public domain
- Appropriate Creative Commons license
- Stock license

Store:
- Creator/photographer
- Source
- License
- Credit requirement
- Original URL where policy permits
- Date obtained
- Usage notes

Never scrape and republish copyrighted medical or editorial images without permission.

For health diagrams, clearly distinguish editorial illustrations from clinical diagnostic imagery.
