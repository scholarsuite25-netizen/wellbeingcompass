# Medical Safety & Editorial Policy

## Purpose
WellMind Health publishes educational health information. Content must support health literacy without pretending to provide individualized medical diagnosis or treatment.

## Required safeguards
Health articles should contain, when relevant:
- General-information disclaimer
- Medical review status
- Last reviewed date
- Sources/references
- Warning signs
- Professional help guidance
- Emergency guidance for urgent topics

## High-risk topics
Require enhanced editorial and medical review for:
- Suicide and self-harm
- Eating disorders
- Abuse
- Severe mental illness
- Medication
- Pregnancy complications
- Emergency symptoms
- Serious disease
- Substance-use emergencies
- Children's health
- Medical procedures

## Language
Use:
- Person-first, respectful language
- Non-stigmatizing descriptions
- Calm and supportive wording
- Clear distinctions between symptoms and diagnosis

Avoid:
- “Guaranteed cure”
- “Miracle treatment”
- “Doctors don't want you to know”
- Unverified supplements as treatments
- Fear-based messaging
- Shame
- Blame
- Absolute promises

## Corrections
When a factual or medical error is discovered:
1. Assess severity.
2. Correct promptly.
3. Record the correction.
4. Re-review affected content.
5. Escalate serious errors to editorial leadership.

## Emergency content
Emergency guidance should direct readers toward appropriate local emergency services, crisis resources, or qualified healthcare professionals. The website must not imply that reading an article is an adequate response to an emergency.
