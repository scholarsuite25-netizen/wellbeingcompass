# Design System

## Brand
WellMind Health

## Colours
Use blue as the primary brand family and yellow as the accent.

Suggested starting tokens:
- Primary blue: #1565C0
- Deep blue: #0D2A4A
- Soft blue: #EAF3FB
- Accent yellow: #F4C542
- Warm background: #FAFCFE
- Text: #17212B
- Muted text: #5E6B76
- Border: #D9E2EA
- Success: #2E7D32
- Warning/error colours must not depend on yellow.

Validate all final combinations for WCAG contrast.

## Typography
Use a highly readable sans-serif for body text. Use a complementary display face sparingly for editorial headings if accessibility and performance remain strong.

## Editorial style
Use:
- Large readable body text
- 1.6–1.8 line height
- Short paragraphs
- Generous margins
- Clear subheadings
- High-quality imagery
- Captions where useful
